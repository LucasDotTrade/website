The operating loop

Every message resolves into state.

Lucas is not another checklist. It is a control plane for the live transaction: channel artifact, private review target, audited outcome.

01 / Channel artifact

The customer sees a concise answer in the thread.

Lucas names the documents reviewed, separates blocked from needs-confirmation, and gives the next action without dumping internal review machinery into WhatsApp or email.

02 / Private review

The operator gets the exact correction target.

Each actionable note or blocker can point to a private row where the team corrects, accepts, waives, or overrides with the source evidence still attached.

03 / Outcome memory

The bank decision closes the loop.

Forward the bank acceptance, rejection, or amendment request. Lucas records it against the transaction instead of letting the outcome vanish in the inbox.

What survives the chat

Corrections become memory.

Trade operations are full of decisions that matter later. Lucas keeps those decisions append-only and tied to the documents that caused them.

Correction

A wrong value gets fixed once.

The accepted correction is recorded with its source and used when Lucas renders the next artifact.

Waiver

A bank-permitted exception stays visible.

Presentation-period waivers, charter party clauses, typo acceptance, and tolerance clauses remain part of the file.

Override

A human decision is not hidden.

When the team decides to proceed anyway, Lucas keeps the rationale with the transaction history.

Outcome

The bank reply closes the evidence loop.

Approval, rejection, amendment request, and final presentation result become outcome rows instead of loose emails.
